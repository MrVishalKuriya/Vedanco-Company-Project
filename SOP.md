# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) / Project Synopsis
## Project Name: Vedanco - Swadeshi Roots, Global Reach

### 1. Proposed System
The proposed system is a centralized web-based platform designed to showcase Vedanco's products and brand identity to a global audience. It serves as a digital bridge between traditional Indian craftsmanship and international markets.

**Key Functions:**
*   **Product Display & Categorization**: Systematically organized listing of products (Handmade, Khadi, Organic, Ayurvedic).
*   **Product Details**: Comprehensive information on natural and Ayurvedic products, highlighting ingredients and health benefits.
*   **Customer Support**: Integrated inquiry system allowing customers to contact the business directly.
*   **Brand Identity**: A dedicated space to share the "Swadeshi Roots, Global Reach" vision and story.
*   **Global Reach**: Optimized for online visibility to attract international distributors and customers.
*   **User Experience**: A responsive, user-friendly interface ensuring seamless browsing across devices.

### 2. Objectives
*   To promote Indian heritage products globally.
*   To provide a seamless platform for product discovery and inquiries.
*   To build trust and credibility through a professional digital presence.
*   To eliminate the limitations of physical marketing and manual catalog distribution.

### 3. Advantages of the System
*   **Brand Visibility**: Expands reach to national and international customers.
*   **24/7 Availability**: Ensures product information is accessible round-the-clock.
*   **Trust & Credibility**: Enhances brand reputation with a professional web presence.
*   **Better Product Presentation**: High-quality visuals and detailed descriptions improve customer understanding.
*   **Customer Engagement**: Facilitates easy communication via contact forms and inquiry buttons.
*   **Efficiency**: Reduces manual effort in sharing product details and catalogs.
*   **Digital Growth**: Lays the foundation for SEO, digital marketing, and future e-commerce expansion.

### 4. Technology Stack

#### Frontend (Client-Side)
*   **HTML5**: For semantic page structure.
*   **CSS3**: Vanilla CSS for custom, responsive styling (Flexbox/Grid).
*   **JavaScript (ES6+)**: For client-side logic and interactivity.
*   **React.js**: Component-based library for building a dynamic user interface.
*   **React Icons**: For lightweight, scalable vector icons.
*   **Vite**: Next-generation frontend tooling for fast builds.

#### Backend (Server-Side)
*   **Node.js**: JavaScript runtime for executing backend code.
*   **Express.js**: Web framework for building robust API endpoints.

#### Database
*   **MongoDB**: NoSQL database for storing contact form submissions and future product data.

#### Development Tools
*   **Figma**: For UI/UX design and prototyping.
*   **VS Code**: Integrated Development Environment (IDE).
*   **Git & GitHub**: Version control and collaboration.
*   **Postman/Curl**: For API testing and verification.

### 5. Future Scope
*   Integration of a full-fledged E-commerce module (Cart, Checkout, Payment Gateway).
*   Admin Dashboard for managing products and viewing inquiries.
*   User Authentication for personalized customer experiences.
*   Multi-language support for diverse global markets.
